A gradient is essentially the equivalent of [[id:50396CED-7F80-4745-B660-EC2277B3D3CF][derivatives]] in higher-dimensional functions. While the derivative $f'\left(a\right)$ is a scalar describing the slope of a curve $f\left(x\right)$ at $a$, the gradient $\nabla f\left(a\right)$ is a vector pointing in the direction of /steepest ascent/. In other words, this is the direction that the curve has the greatest increase. Similarly, the negative gradient $- \nabla f\left(a\right)$ points towards the steepest downwards direction.
